Board Resolution Formatting Rules:
1. Page: A4, margins 25.4 mm on all sides.
2. Title: Times New Roman, 16 pt, bold, centered.
3. Body: Times New Roman, 12 pt, justified, first-line indent 2 characters, line spacing 1.5.
4. Signature block: Times New Roman, 12 pt, right-aligned.
5. Date line: Times New Roman 12 pt, right-aligned.
